Silent Hill 2

The more I read books, the more I find video game stories average. They aren’t bad, but the gameplay phases break the rhythm of the story and limit its development. If I have to read notes to understand the world, I prefer a real book where I am not active and the story is well-built.

Silent Hill 2 is too ‘cryptic’ for me. The older I get, the more I hate searching for clues on pieces of paper or on walls. I want a game that is more like a movie and tells me where to go.

A game needs a balance. If the story is short, the gameplay should be short too. If the gameplay is long (and in this case, repetitive) but the story is hidden in small notes, it just becomes boring.

The graphics and the music are great. The atmosphere is amazing, even if it gets a bit repetitive after a while. James is a cool character and the acting is great. But the game is too repetitive and the story feels like ‘déjà-vu.’ If you are not a nostalgic fan, the game feels a bit old and too slow.
