Metal Gear Solid 3 - Debriefing

Like with many video game soundtracks, knowing the story changes everything — if you’ve played the game and understand the context, the music takes on a completely different meaning.

It’s no longer just notes; it becomes a memory of a tragedy that resonates deeply.

Even though The Boss remains calm and composed throughout the story, the ending is incredibly moving and heavy. She doesn’t say much, but you can feel the weight of everything she’s chosen to bear — and the quiet tragedy behind it. (Getting a bit carried away there?)
