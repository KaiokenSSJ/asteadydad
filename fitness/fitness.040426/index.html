Skinny Fat to Fit: Starting with a Cut

My performance has improved a lot on certain exercises. I don’t really like giving specific examples — it kills my motivation with all that comparison, and that’s just not the point.

Anyway, I’m feeling good and full of energy right now.

I can’t wait to see it start showing in the mirror though, because cutting isn’t exactly fun… I’m not gaining any mass in a deficit (obviously), and even if the body fat seems to be dropping very, very slowly according to the scale trend (the curve over several days, not the daily number), nothing’s visible yet.

I wonder if I’ll manage to hit that fateful 15% body fat that fitness sites always recommend. No rush though — it’s not a race, as long as I keep the pace :)
